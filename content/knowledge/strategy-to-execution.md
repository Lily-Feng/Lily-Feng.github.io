Strategy is not a list of initiatives. It is a connected set of choices about where to play, how to win, what capabilities must exist, and what the organization will deliberately not do.

## Translating a strategic bet

For each bet, make four things explicit:

- **Outcome:** What observable change makes this worth doing?
- **Capability:** What must the organization become reliably good at?
- **Sequence:** Which assumptions need to be tested before scaling investment?
- **Signal:** What evidence would make us continue, change direction, or stop?

This translation keeps a roadmap from becoming a backlog of unrelated requests. Teams can connect delivery work to the capability being built and leaders can revisit the assumptions behind the investment.
