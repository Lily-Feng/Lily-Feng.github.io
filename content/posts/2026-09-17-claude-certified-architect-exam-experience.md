Found the Claude Certified Architect – Professional (CCAR-P) exam about a week ago and just went for it with minimum prep — I used Claude to generate some practice questions beforehand and was scoring 100% on those, so I figured I was ready.

The real exam was a different level of difficulty, leaning entirely on past architecture work to get through it. What surprised me is how much you learn just by taking it — it's less "test what you know" and more "discover what you don't."

Besides solution design, multi-agent systems, and evaluation frameworks, there's also observability at scale, lifecycle management, and accuracy/latency trade-offs — areas that I guess Anthropic took a lot of learning from failure to put it in the certificate.
