Agentic payment is the emerging stack that allows AI agents to safely discover merchants, negotiate transactions, receive delegated authority from users, and execute payments across traditional and programmable rails. It is not just “AI checkout.” It combines agent communication, identity, trust, mandate collection, transaction coordination, payment authentication, settlement, and post-transaction accountability.

The core shift is from human-clicked payments to policy-bound, cryptographically verifiable agent actions. In this new model, the agent does not simply autofill a checkout page. It must prove who it is, what user or business authorized it, what constraints it is operating under, what it is buying, and why the transaction should be trusted by merchants, networks, and payment providers.

Different standards are forming around different layers. MCP and A2A help agents talk to tools, merchants, and other agents. AP2 defines how humans delegate payment authority. ACP and UCP coordinate commerce flows between assistants and merchants. x402, MPP, and ATXP push toward machine-speed payments for APIs, compute, data, and MCP tools. Visa Intelligent Commerce, Visa TAP, and Mastercard Agent Pay bring network-backed trust, credential binding, authentication, and liability models.

The important point is that no single protocol owns the whole stack yet. Agentic payment will likely be a layered ecosystem where open agent protocols, payment networks, merchant standards, programmable money, and risk systems compete and compose. The winners will be the systems that make agent actions discoverable, authorized, auditable, reversible when needed, and trusted at scale.
