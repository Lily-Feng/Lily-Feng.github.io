Teams learn continuously, but that learning often remains trapped in meetings, pull requests, and the memories of a few people. A learning loop makes the result reusable.

## Capture the smallest useful artifact

After an important decision or incident, write down:

1. the context and constraint,
2. the choice that was made,
3. the evidence behind it,
4. what happened next, and
5. when the decision should be revisited.

The artifact does not need to be long. It needs to be findable and connected to the system, project, or principle it informs. Over time, those connections become more valuable than any single document.
